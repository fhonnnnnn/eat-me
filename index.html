HOW TO PLAY

FruitNIBBLE Ice cream & snacksSNACK FriesBIG BITE Fried chickenFEAST
  • Eat food to grow. Only the bigger bubble can eat the other one, so if your rival is bigger, run.
  • The % over each bubble is its size compared with its rival. At 115% it turns green and can eat. Under 87% it turns red: run!
  • A round ends when someone gets eaten. After 60 seconds the bigger bubble takes the round. Win 2 of 3 rounds to win.
  • Move with WASD or arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere to steer.
  • On iPhone, switch off Silent mode to hear the munching.
