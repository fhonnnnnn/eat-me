HOW TO PLAY

Fruit+1 Ice cream & snacks+2 Fries+3 Fried chicken+4
  • Everyone starts at 1. Eat food and your number goes up.
  • Bigger number eats smaller number! 5 EAT! green means go get them. 3 RUN! red means run away. Same number? You just bounce.
  • A round ends when someone gets eaten. After 60 seconds the bigger number wins the round. Win 2 of 3 rounds to win.
  • Move with WASD or arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere to steer.
  • On iPhone, switch off Silent mode to hear the munching.
